# Figure sources

`figure-1.png` and `sampling.png` reproduce Figures 1 and 2 from the authors'
CORDS manuscript, *Continuous Representations of Discrete Structures*, ICLR 2026.
The paper is available at [arXiv:2601.21583](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21583).

The images were rendered from the supplied `CORDS___ICLR_2026.pdf`, pages 2 and
5 respectively. Page headers, surrounding body text, and printed captions were
excluded; the README supplies captions in text. The figure contents are
unchanged. Figure 1 uses the PDF rectangle `(173, 80, 440, 310)` at 4× resolution;
Figure 2 uses `(109, 78, 504, 272)` at 3× resolution (PDF points, top-left origin).

`molecules.png` and `mnist.png` are previews generated by the repository's
notebooks from the bundled QM9 and MNIST examples.
